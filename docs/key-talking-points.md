# Key Talking Points

Use these points to keep the interview conversation focused.

## Lead With

- Patterns are selected from change pressure, not added for decoration.
- Command, State, and Specification are the strongest examples to discuss.
- Tests prove behavior and make design trade-offs easier to inspect.

## Go Deeper On

- When a pattern reduces complexity.
- When a pattern becomes overengineering.
- How small examples can still show senior design judgment.

## Avoid Overclaiming

- Do not imply patterns are always better.
- Explain the context, trade-off, and simpler alternative.
